Documentation

For the fifth and final element, I played around with the Village Scene and made my own alterations to it, making it truly different than intended. I made my own houses with different colours and a new roof texture. The ground itself has changed, the grass and having been given a checkerboard pattern. Most prominantly, the lay of the land has also changed. Using a new heightmap, based on the map of "Kronos Island" from "Sonic Frontiers", the ground warps and wraps to a new shape entirely different from the original village map. In order to have the houses lay to rest upon the hills, I had to play with the heights and width of both the heightmap and the houses.
